# The humanoid robot field, 2026

A market snapshot of 30 prominent humanoid robots across five countries.
The website version is interactive: filter by country and commercial
availability, search the full dataset, sort the major market columns, and
hover over each robot for a larger image.

## Classification

- **Available** means a sales, preorder, reservation, leasing, or paid
  deployment channel exists.
- **Announced** means the robot is a prototype, closed pilot, or announced
  product without an open commercial path.
- An asterisk marks an estimate, target, third-party report, or
  portfolio-wide number.
- Unknown prices and unit counts are not treated as zero and sort last.

## Reading the field

The table is a market map, not a leaderboard. “Available” covers several
very different levels of maturity: a reservation, a research unit, and a
robot performing paid warehouse work all have a commercial path, but they do
not have equal evidence of reliability.

Unit counts are especially difficult to compare. Companies variously report
model deliveries, portfolio totals, pilots, orders, production capacity, or
nothing. Portfolio totals are retained because they signal manufacturing
scale, but they are explicitly marked.
