## Reading the table

The best product is conditional on the curriculum. Camera cars preserve the
core perception-and-control loop at low cost. TurtleBots justify their price
when ROS navigation is the actual subject. Arms are the correct apparatus for
manipulation and imitation learning. Legged systems only earn their additional
complexity when gait, balance, or locomotion is itself the learning objective.
